## Use sentence

The dataset authors write that the web-scraping policies of the news sources were checked where those policies were available, and that the content can be used for non-commercial research only by crediting the news source.

## Project use

Stage 1 of this project may use the Kaggle copy for non-commercial research. Credit the news source named in the file, and credit the authors' Mendeley release. This file does not authorize collecting new rows from ARY, Express, Tribune, Dawn, Geo, or Aaj.
